age-mail

Send anonymous & confidential information to people who made their age public key public.

Uses age encryption. For this see the age-mail project.

Before you send

  • The recipient's email address is visible to the operator of this server. Address lookup and delivery require it.
  • The message content and your identity as sender are not visible to the operator. Encryption happens in your browser; the server only relays ciphertext.
  • This means the operator can see who you send to, but not what you send or who you are. Choose a server you are comfortable telling that to.
  • The operator should only be trusted to a limited extent — they cannot read your message, but they could log recipient addresses, observe traffic patterns, or be compelled to do so. Don't pick a server you would not pick to handle the metadata about your conversations.
Recipients must have their age public key published. Entered recipients will be checked if a public key is published.

    Setup Recipient Age Public Key

    Publish your age public key via DNS TXT record or HTTPS. See the GitHub repository for full details.

    DNS TXT record

    dig +short TXT _age.your-domain.com
"your-domain.com;dave@your-domain.com;age1a3xsw5j5d27k4zmp5wzr7kp49m7gvgt87f32gq3he7da0r4jne6qyk4mmy"

    HTTPS

    https://your-domain.com/.well-known/age
